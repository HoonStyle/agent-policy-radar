# Product-specific art direction

## Start from what the screen helps someone do

Identify the unit of work, information relationships, frequency, density, and consequence of a mistake. Establish what must dominate the first viewport and what can recede. Keep observed product facts separate from design hypotheses.

When exploring a substantial new direction, compare two plausible compositions using the same real content. Record the navigation model, content hierarchy, density, focal point, and trade-off of each. Color-only variants are not distinct concepts. Do not force two concepts when the user already selected one or asked for a small fix.

## Compose a visual language

- **Type:** Pair roles, not merely font names. Establish display/body/data/label relationships through size, weight, line-height, and measure. Check available glyphs and licenses; avoid font downloads as a default.
- **Composition:** Choose a grid and optical anchors. Vary emphasis without making every section compete. Whitespace must clarify relationships, not create empty hero acreage above the task.
- **Color/material:** Use a coherent neutral temperature and accent logic. Read `color-system.md` to map roles, states, and contrast. Assess color area in the rendered screen, not just isolated chips. Depth and texture can be appropriate when they express the product.
- **Images/graphics:** Decide what each asset communicates, its crop, and its visual family. Reuse approved assets first. Leave absent imagery absent rather than inserting unrelated stock art. Source or generate new assets only within the requested scope, identifying provenance.
- **Motion:** Tie changes to orientation, continuity, feedback, or deliberate expression. Decide duration/easing from the interaction rather than adding the same entrance animation everywhere. Respect reduced motion.
- **Finish:** Align glyphs optically; check icon stroke weight, baselines, line ends, borders, selected states, hover, and focus together.

For a moodboard, prefer a few inspected references selected for different reasons. Record `URL → observed principle → adaptation → what does not fit`. A gallery listing is a discovery source, not proof that its designs are usable or reusable. Keep any uninspected reference explicitly provisional.

## Avoid replacing one default with another

A flat cream-and-serif layout can be just as generic as a dark card dashboard. Ask: would changing only the logo make this look like an unrelated product? If so, revisit content hierarchy and interaction before ornament. Conversely, do not erase familiar controls just for novelty.

Validate with the main task and actual viewport constraints. A chosen direction is ready to extend only when it is visually inspectable and its primary action remains clear.
